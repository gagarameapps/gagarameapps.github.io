Privacy Policy for Rafter Pro Offline

Effective date: October 15, 2026

Rafter Pro Offline ("the App") is developed by Gagarame. This policy explains how the App handles your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The App does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, location, or contacts.

Data stored on your device

Any data you enter in the App (such as saved records, history and settings) is stored only locally on your own device. It is never uploaded to us or to any server. Uninstalling the App deletes this data.

Internet access, analytics and advertising

The core features of the App work fully offline. The App contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party data-collection SDKs.

Sharing and exporting

If you choose to export or share content from the App, it is passed directly to the app or service you select on your device. We never receive a copy.

Purchases

The App is free to download and offers an optional one-time in-app purchase to unlock all features. Payments are processed entirely by Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive or store your payment details. Your unlock status is stored on your device and can be restored through the store's "Restore Purchases" function.

Permissions

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos unless a feature clearly requires it and you grant permission yourself.

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
